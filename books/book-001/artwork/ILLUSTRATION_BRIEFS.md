The current layout draws images inside a 507 × 435 point box, preserving aspect ratio and centering inside the page safe area. At that box the candidates are ~217 PPI, below 300. To fill a 435-point height at 300 PPI would require at least 1813 native vertical pixels, and width must independently meet 300 PPI for its actual placement. Upscaling, relabeling DPI and metadata do not create detail. A smaller actual placement could meet 300 PPI but would need separate age-fit/colouring-space and design approval; do not silently shrink.
