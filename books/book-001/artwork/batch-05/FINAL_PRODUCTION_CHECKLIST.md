# Final production checklist

## Blocking before publication

- Verify all 150 scientific checks and every activity claim, sex/form, anatomical mark, lookalike and local/seasonal caveat; resolve B08 source access and obtain a knowledgeable human review.
- Resolve identification and colouring defects listed in QC_REPORT.md, especially B22, B20, B21 and the previous-batch backlog. Separately approve all artwork and updated activities.
- Check vector paths and original-to-vector comparisons, closed colouring regions, edges and effective line thickness by printing at actual size. Verify fonts are licensed and embedded. Do not count vectorization or automation as proof of print quality.
- Design and lay out revised activities; review with children/caregivers for age suitability and sufficient working space. Build and proof all 64 pages including introduction, observation log, answers and source notes. Confirm facing pages, gutters, bleed and safe margins against current official KDP guidance.
- Prepare original cover art and typography using current KDP cover calculator dimensions. Confirm title, legibility, spine eligibility and rights.
- Recheck current KDP content rules, AI-generated-content disclosure and pricing/royalties for both target marketplaces. Verify cost assumptions; do not claim demand or profit is established.
- Obtain explicit user approvals before KDP upload, physical proof order, spending or publication. After authorization, inspect KDP Print Previewer and a physical proof for trimming, gutters, tonal quality, registration and colouring usability; correct failures and approve the final files separately.

## Minor preferences

Nonessential pose, decorative detail or stylistic differences can be deferred unless they interfere with identification, usability or print quality. Do not regenerate accepted art for cosmetic consistency alone.
