The coarse connected-component check found 34 enclosed white regions of at least 25 source pixels in the thresholded source and 32 in the trace; this does not by itself prove a practical colouring problem. Inspect the toes, branch and tiny enclosed gaps at high magnification and actual print scale. Thresholding, rasterization and connectivity heuristics can also affect this result. No automated topology count establishes that every necessary colouring region is closed.

A distance-transform local-maximum screen found 3.93% of sampled maxima below the externally supplied 0.75 pt figure at the revised 435 pt placement. This is a screening signal, not the fraction of all strokes that fail and not an exact measurement: endpoints, antialiasing and corners can trigger false positives. SVG line art is filled contour geometry rather than uniform-width strokes, so the minimum thickness is **not verified**. Check printed line ends, fine wing and toe details, and any broken contours manually. At contact-sheet scale curves look smooth; that cannot establish printed performance.
