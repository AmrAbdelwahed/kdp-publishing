# Backyard Bird Detectives: Color, Spot & Explore
**Provisional editorial draft — owner approval and evidence review required.** Based only on the supplied brief; no market validation or species facts have been independently verified in this task.

## Value proposition
A guided sequence for ages 6–9: colour a clear original bird portrait, inspect a verified identification clue, then practice a short observation skill. Parents, grandparents and educators can facilitate without needing specialist knowledge. Thirty paired lessons build one coherent experience; no claim that field marks, facts or checklists are new inventions. Candidate opportunities are hypotheses until competitor review.

## Structure and learning progression
Pages 1–2 introduce the method and safe observation; pages 3–62 alternate colouring and corresponding activity; pages 63–64 hold a reusable observation log and verified source/answer notes. See PAGE_PLAN.csv for every page. Odd pages are recto; even pages verso. Every colouring reverse is an activity page. A facing spread is not promised for every pair. Use pencils/crayons for the draft positioning; test stock before any medium claims. No single-sided, blank-reverse or marker-bleed-proof claims.

Each activity page should contain: common name, verified reference thumbnail or key if needed, one short checked field-mark prompt, one task taking roughly 5–10 minutes (editorial estimate to test), and an optional observation extension. Do not ask children to identify by colour alone. Include not seen / unsure options; an outdoor sighting is never necessary to complete the activity. Adult reads prompts where needed. Test vocabulary and task length with target-age readers before freezing.

## Species scope
SPECIES_LIST.csv lists 30 candidate species, not a universal local checklist. Some are regionally restricted, seasonal, introduced or visually confusable; specific statuses must be checked. Reject the implication that every North American yard has all 30. Regional coverage and lookalikes require expert/source review; substitute species only with a logged editorial decision and updated pagination. No range, conservation or feeding claims are approved. Select a documented representative plumage/view per plate and label sex/season/form only after verification.

## Accuracy and safety
Check current common/scientific names, proportional anatomy, visible toes, bill, wing attachment, feather groups, tail and eye placement against reliable references. Do not infer absent anatomy from a generated picture. Identification keys and answer choices must be independently checked. Avoid nest approach, handling, feeding, playback, road crossings or approaching wildlife; observe quietly from a safe spot with an adult, respect private property and local rules. Avoid collecting child names/addresses/precise sighting locations.
