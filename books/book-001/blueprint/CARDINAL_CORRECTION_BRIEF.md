## ChatGPT image-generation brief
Create one original, black-and-white, portrait-format children's colouring illustration of a Northern Cardinal in a relaxed side view, perched on a simple branch. Show the entire bird and branch inside a clear safe area. Choose one specific sex and plumage only after checking the Cornell identification page and an appropriate reference. Keep a clear pointed crest and a short, deep conical bill, verify the facial mask around bill/eyes and throat against the chosen reference, and preserve natural relative bill, head, body, wing, tail and leg proportions. Render toes gripping the branch naturally, with the correct number and orientation established by reference; no fused claws, extra limbs or branch lines crossing the feet. Wing/flight feathers should attach naturally and remain distinguishable; the long tail must not merge into the branch. Avoid copying a reference photo, Cornell image or competitor art.

Use white paper, solid black smooth outlines, no gray, text, logos or background hatching. Provide generous enclosed spaces for 6–9-year-olds. Proposed final-size contour 1–1.5 pt; secondary lines at least 0.5 pt, subject to print review. Minimal background, preferably two large leaves, with no elements near trim/gutter. Do not fill the mask solid if this conceals details; outline the reference-checked mask for colouring. No visual claim or labeling is approved until checked.

For a final raster image require actual native pixel dimensions at least 300 times the final placed width and height in inches, measured in both axes (full trim would require 2550 × 3300 pixels). Vector artwork must be genuinely resolution independent and reviewed for line weight. Inspect output at final size for broken outlines, squeezed spaces and anatomy; retain source, generation and approval records. This brief does not authorize a 30-image run.
