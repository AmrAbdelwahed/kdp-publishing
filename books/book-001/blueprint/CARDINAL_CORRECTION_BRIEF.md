## Brief for a later image-generation session
Create original black-and-white portrait-format line art for a children's colouring page, ages 6–9. One adult male Northern Cardinal in a natural side-on pose, perched on a simple twig. Use an independently reviewed Northern Cardinal reference for proportions, prominent crest, short deep conical bill, facial mask boundaries, wing feathers, long tail and feet gripping the branch. The species, reference source, sex and any depicted seasonal differences must be confirmed by a knowledgeable reviewer. Draw clean, consistent outlines, generous colourable areas, no grey, hatching, dense detail, text or decorative frame. Keep the full bird and branch clear of all print margins. Retain accurate facial-mask boundary without using large filled areas that conceal anatomy. Use two simple leaves away from the edges. Do not copy a photograph or another book's artwork.

Output must be reviewed against references, not accepted from the prompt alone. For eventual print use native dimensions adequate for its actual placement (minimum 300 ppi; full trim 2550 × 3300 pixels) or a genuinely suitable vector original. Verify actual print-size stroke weights and page placement. No full art set authorized by this brief.
