# Cover concept evaluation — subjective review

These are three AI-generated front-cover illustrations, composed with separately typeset exact text. The [full-size sheet](full-size-comparison.png) and [185-pixel-wide thumbnail simulation](thumbnail-comparison.png) are design reviews, not buyer testing or Amazon listing screenshots. All three show an adult male cardinal; A also shows an American Robin. Actual artwork, creative direction and typography have not received human or commercial approval.

**Recommendation: B for further development**, subject to the user's selection. Its detective props provide the strongest immediate link to the proposed educational activity. Before considering it final, make the colouring/activity cue clearer at small sizes, check the bird scientifically and prepare genuinely sufficient-resolution, rights-cleared artwork with the official wrap template. Its detailed color painting differs from the line-art interior; avoid implying that inside pages are colored. The blank badges are decorative rather than earned credentials.

All three are RGB screen concepts below KDP's 300-DPI minimum at full-cover placement; merely enlarging or changing metadata will not fix this. Color conversion, bleed, safe margins, barcode, final author details and trim are untested. Compare species with the [Cornell Northern Cardinal ID page](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Northern_Cardinal/id) and [American Robin ID page](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/American_Robin/id), retrieved for Milestone 8B on 2026-11-15; scientific and anatomical approval is pending. Human reviewers should check toes, feet, beaks, feather boundaries and the relationship between the cardinal's face and throat on each concept. No conclusions about conversion or sales have been measured.
