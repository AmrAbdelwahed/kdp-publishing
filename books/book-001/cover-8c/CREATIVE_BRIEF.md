# Creative brief — working concept

**Backyard Bird Detectives: Color, Spot & Explore** is a 64-page black-and-white colouring and identification activity book for children ages 6–9; parents, grandparents and educators are likely buyers. Thirty bird lessons connect colour, identification clues and optional observation. Birds vary by region and season. The interior is printed on both sides; do not promise single-sided pages or marker bleed protection.

The front should make the title instantly legible, suggest that this is an engaging colouring/activity book and invite a child into real bird exploration. The concepts explore three original directions: A, friendly backyard adventure; B, junior detective/field notebook; C, premium nature illustration. They are illustration and typography studies, not final wraps. Keep individual birds anatomically plausible and clearly distinct from copyrighted characters or other publishers' covers. Avoid unsupported learning outcomes, guaranteed sightings, fake endorsements and unverified sales claims.

Color covers are concept art, while the actual interior uses black-and-white line drawings and structured activities. Do not suggest the interior is in color. The birds, style, title, ages, exact positioning and all fonts/illustration rights require review before finalization. Source art and all older assets remain unchanged.
