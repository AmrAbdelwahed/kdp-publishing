# Cover review — B1 and B2

**Recommendation: B1 for further development.** Its bold sans-serif title and dark category banner have slightly stronger contrast at the 185-pixel simulation. B2's serif title and ochre banner are quieter and more premium, but its category line is less distinct at small sizes. Both simplify the shields, retain a cardinal, magnifier and smaller binoculars, and give the category more visual emphasis. These are design judgments, not measured customer preferences, sales or conversion claims. The subtitle and category are still relatively small at thumbnail size; assess on real devices before finalizing.
