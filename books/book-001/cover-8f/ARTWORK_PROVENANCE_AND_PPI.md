# B1 artwork provenance and effective resolution

**The review wrap is not print-ready.** It is intentionally rendered pixel-identically to the approved 8E review layout. Its only placed raster is the original unresampled B1 detail crop, 1102 × 903 native pixels over 8.5 × 6.9608 in: **129.65 × 129.73 effective PPI**. At 300 PPI this slot needs at least **2550 × 2089 genuinely detailed native pixels**. The cardinal, magnifier, notebook, binoculars and textured background remain flattened together in that raster; they have not been separated or recreated as high-resolution assets. Changing DPI tags, interpolation or sharpening cannot meet this detail requirement.

`B1-vector-base-ARTWORK-MISSING.pdf` is a separately saved, guide-free **incomplete** version of the same layout with the entire detailed raster omitted. Its background fields, panels, banners and typography are vector, as is the existing B01 back-cover line-art inset. It has no raster images. This is a useful reconstruction base, not a finished cover. The gold magnifier rim, notebook, binoculars and cardinal are **not yet independently rebuilt**; their interlocking edges, texture and depth make a blind auto-trace unsuitable. A careful vector reconstruction could cover simple gold rim bands, notebook outline, background curves and binocular shapes; feather detail, eye, feet, foliage, lighting and lens reflections require detailed artwork. Blend and occlusion must be reviewed at print size.

Three native-pixel crops are supplied solely as **visual references**, not transparent cutouts or usable 300-PPI assets. Their overlapping reference windows include background and other objects. `REFERENCE_SLOTS.json` records source pixel rectangles, SHA-256 hashes, top-left placement measured in inches from the trimmed front's upper-left, physical sizes and the minimum native pixel dimensions for each window. Those numbers apply to the entire specified window, including any transparent padding. Do not stack these crops as if they were isolated artwork. The complete scene starts at front-trim x=0, y≈3.3146 in and occupies 8.5 × 6.9608 in. The [exact ChatGPT prompts](CHATGPT_ASSET_PROMPTS.md) offer a single-scene path and an optional layered path.

Original B1 is an OpenAI image-edited candidate using the prior Concept B as visual reference; the retained B1 source and original template are unchanged. See [8D provenance](../cover-8d/ARTWORK_PROVENANCE.json) and [8E font and rights record](../cover-8e/FONT_AND_RIGHTS.md). The Cardinal ID page was rechecked in 8E on 2026-11-15; the red plumage, crest, thick bill and dark face are broad source-supported cues. Human expert review of anatomy, originality, license and AI disclosure remains pending. Font subsets are embedded DejaVu; no Helvetica resource appears in the wrap. The DejaVu license notice remains available in [the 8B license record](../interior-8b/FONT_LICENSE.txt); final rights review is pending.
