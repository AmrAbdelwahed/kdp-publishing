## What is verified

- [KDP paperback submission guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950), retrieved 17 November 2026 UTC, specify for black-and-white white-paper interiors a spine of pages × 0.002252 in and 0.125 in external bleed. Thus 64 pages gives 0.144128 in spine and `(0.125 + 8.5 + 0.144128 + 8.5 + 0.125) × (0.125 + 11 + 0.125)` = **17.394128 × 11.25 in**. The selected PDF MediaBox is 1252.376953 × 810 pt, consistent within PDF serialization with 1252.377216 × 810 pt. The supplied PDF template labels 17.394 × 11.250 in and draws to x = 1252.3772 pt, but its MediaBox ends at 1252 pt. The discrepancy is **0.3772 pt (0.00524 in)**. Why the template box is shorter is unknown; do not silently crop. The mathematical reconstruction is supported; KDP acceptance of either box is not established. A fresh template or KDP clarification and later Print Previewer check remain necessary.
- The earlier template overlay and current automated geometry checks place text inside the designated areas, with no spine text and the 2 × 1.2 in back barcode zone clear of essential content. Background reaches the page edges. Decorative front detail, especially the magnifier handle, approaches the fold/trim zone; inspect against the actual template and in a physical proof. No KDP template guides are intended to appear in the candidate.
- All fonts reported by both review PDFs are embedded DejaVu fonts; there is no Helvetica resource in the selected wrap. Both are unencrypted. The selected cover has one 4408 × 3612 DeviceRGB image at 8.5 × 6.9608 in, about **518.6 × 518.9 output-sampling PPI**. Its preserved original 1102 × 903 scene was about **129.7 PPI** at the same placement. The extra pixels are inferred by the AI model, not recovered source detail. No image soft mask was found in the image report; that limited check is not a full transparency or print validation.
- The actual current interior contains 64 letter-size single pages. Its 30 claims and answer-key checks are recorded separately in `CLAIM_AND_ANSWER_REVIEW.csv`. Prior and current tests check page sequence, font embedding, margins and the unchanged activity pages; physical line strength and usability are not established.

## Still open before final export

1. **Color and cover fidelity.** [KDP cover help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020), retrieved 17 November 2026 UTC, says images should be CMYK, discourages multiple color spaces and says embedded color profiles are removed. The downloaded template readme asks for a CMYK profile; the current candidate is DeviceRGB. These instructions are not fully reconciled. Preserve the approved RGB source and produce a separately reviewed CMYK candidate using an appropriate print workflow; confirm the profile question with KDP and inspect actual color in Previewer and proof. No blind conversion or color approval is claimed.
2. **Submission cleanup.** Current KDP paperback guidelines say submitted PDFs should not contain metadata, invisible objects or annotations and should flatten transparency. Both review PDFs have descriptive document metadata. The revised interior's pages 18 and 20 retain the old illustration forms as unused resources, although those forms are no longer drawn. Determine how KDP treats such unused resources and produce a clean export without metadata, unused objects or annotations, then verify that its pages remain visually identical. Do not change the existing review files in place. A PDF image report alone cannot prove every transparency or clipping interaction is safe.
3. **Human checks.** Resolve the prioritized field-mark and coloring ambiguities, remaining scientific/age/regional and artwork-rights questions, and test small coloring spaces and cover reconstruction at physical size. See `ART_AND_EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md` and `RIGHTS_AND_AI_DISCLOSURE.md`. User approval of the cover direction and woodpecker candidates is not expert, physical-print or publication approval.
4. **KDP and proof.** When separately authorized, check the finished files in KDP Print Previewer for trim, gutter, bleed, barcode position, folds, images and warnings. A physical proof later requires separate authorization and expense; examine color, binding, legibility and usability. Neither stage guarantees acceptance or sales.
