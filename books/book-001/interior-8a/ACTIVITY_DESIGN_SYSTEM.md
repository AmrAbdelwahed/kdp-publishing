# Activity design system — review draft

The approved 64-page structure is unchanged. The previous activity pages repeated a clue box, a mission box and an observation box with nearly identical writing areas. `previous-versus-revised.png` shows six matched examples at the same scale. The revision uses six formats, five lessons each; each includes a source-checked clue, a complete in-book task, an answer or key, and a different optional observation prompt.

| Format | Activity pages | Main composition and skill |
|---|---|---|
| Detective challenge | 3, 15, 27, 39, 51 | Three lettered choices beside a small unchanged vector portrait; locate one source-described clue. |
| Pattern comparison | 5, 17, 29, 41, 53 | Side-by-side original conceptual keys for body regions, line positions, wing patterns, head forms or seasons; compare examples. |
| Colour the clues | 7, 19, 31, 43, 55 | Large named swatches, checks, bands or eye circles; use the supplied colour key, with sex/season qualifications. |
| Observation mission | 9, 21, 33, 45, 57 | Imaginary, explicitly labelled field-note cases with visible/hidden clues and three choices; no live bird required. |
| Draw and discover | 11, 23, 35, 47, 59 | Large work area with an abstract labeled pattern or region key to shade, complete or draw. |
| Comparison lab | 13, 25, 37, 49, 61 | Two or three matching lines between source-provided clue and colour cards; the relevant information is printed above. |

The pattern keys are original geometric diagrams, labeled as keys rather than bird portraits. The head-line diagram on page 17 is specifically labeled as a *position* key: the printed black stroke does not represent stripe colour. On pages with unresolved illustration defects, the main exercise relies on its separate, source-backed key and does not assert the disputed drawing is accurate. That mitigation does not close the artwork blockers.

Primary clue and instruction text uses 12.5 pt DejaVu Sans, choices and matching labels about 12 pt, optional observation text 11.5 pt, and answer entries 11.5 pt. Source-index and navigation labels are 9–10 pt. DejaVu Sans and Sans Bold are embedded. The installed font license and notices are preserved as `FONT_LICENSE.txt`; final distribution and credit compliance require review.

Pages retain 8.5 × 11-inch dimensions, no bleed, 45/54 pt mirrored text insets, a consistent footer, and an open writing line for the optional observations. User-selected artwork appears unchanged on the even pages. Five challenge pages reuse those original vector images as small portraits. The welcome page distinguishes imaginary exercises from optional real observations; the log distinguishes real birds from pictures. Page 63 has one answer or pattern guide for every activity. Page 64 provides a printed Cornell site and species index with clickable per-species links in the digital PDF.

This is a design for review, not evidence that children can use the pages without adult help. Some clue-to-answer tasks remain simple, and the six layouts intentionally repeat five times. Test with children ages 6–9 and caregivers; adjust task difficulty, drawing areas, source notes and typography where observation warrants. No claim of market differentiation or final product approval is made from this design alone.
