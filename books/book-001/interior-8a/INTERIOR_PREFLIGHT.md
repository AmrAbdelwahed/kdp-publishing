## Automated and source checks

- The review PDF contains 64 letter-size pages. Page 1 is the introduction; pages 2–61 are 30 even-left illustration/odd-right activity pairs; pages 62–64 contain log, answers and source index. The PDF requests a right-hand first page. All 30 inherited colouring pages render identically to Milestone 7 in the automated comparison.
- The test checks page order, species, displayed claim and instruction text, page numbers, text-box bounds, text-block collisions, 30 unique vector-art hashes, 30 answer entries, six formats, 30 distinct optional prompts, vector paths and absence of embedded raster page images. It checks embedded font programs and minimum extracted text size of 9 pt. ReportLab layout code also rejects paragraph overflow within its reserved box. These checks do not prove that artwork lines, graphics or every visual element are free of overlaps or physically legible.
- `pdffonts` reports embedded DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Sans Bold. License notices are in `FONT_LICENSE.txt`; human legal/credit review is pending. Main child-facing clue/instruction text is 12.5 pt and answer text 11.5 pt; some navigation and source labels are 9–10 pt.
- `ARTWORK_SCREEN.csv` records the actual placed vector-form bounds and an estimate of the original padded raster grid's sampling at this placement: approximately 235.5–250.1 pixels per inch. The final PDF uses vector paths, so this is **not** a DPI value for the SVG and does not prove or disprove KDP acceptance. Tracing cannot create missing original detail.
- Historical coarse region counts differ for 12 selected traces; B25 changes from 29 to 25, and several others differ by one or two. Those screens are not proof of a broken or closed colouring boundary. A new 300-dpi raster screen of the actual art placements flags 1.62–20.5% of local line maxima below the 0.75-point threshold (B13 is highest). The screen thresholds 300-dpi grayscale renders of each placed art area, samples local distance-transform maxima, and converts their approximate widths to points. This is a sampling heuristic affected by fine stems, dots and filled regions—not a percentage of all strokes, proof of a defect, or proof of a 0.75-point minimum. Inspect all key contours, joints, and fine areas on paper at actual size.
- The [official KDP paperback guideline](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950), fetched 2026-11-15 06:19:38 UTC, lists a 0.375-inch inside margin and at least 0.25-inch outside margin for 24–150 pages with no bleed, and says images should be at least 300 DPI. Text bounds and inherited vector-form bounds were screened against a 36-pt minimum; actual graphic placement, font license, current official requirements and raster/vector print rendering still require human verification.

## Material caveats

`ARTWORK_QC_BACKLOG.csv` lists 15 reviewed issues: eight conservative **BLOCKING** classifications, six **ACCEPTABLE_WITH_CAVEAT**, and one **COSMETIC** style concern. These are review triage, not scientific/print approval. No source artwork was edited: the compound paths and anatomical uncertainty made local changes unreliable. Source-backed schematic activities reduce dependency on unclear art but do not repair it.

All 30 activity claims have source-specific records; unrelated anatomy, geography and activity-suitability checks have not been silently promoted to verified. Answer entries exist for all 30 pages, but neither answer clarity nor age suitability has been tested with children. Source labels are smaller than body copy. Print at 100%, review with a caregiver/child and knowledgeable bird reviewer, and resolve the backlog before any KDP upload or proof order.
