# Actual-size review checklist

1. On letter paper, print at **100% / actual size**, not “fit”. For facing spreads, start page 1 on a right-hand side. Check with a ruler that the page is 8.5 × 11 inches; a home printer can clip its own nonprintable edges.
2. Check the page pairs, inner/outer margins, page numbers, page 63 answer key, and page 64 source labels. Read the smallest 9–10 pt labels at a normal distance.
3. Inspect every bird’s face, bill, wings, feet and tail. Compare the eight blocking issues and six caveats in `ARTWORK_QC_BACKLOG.csv` with authoritative references. In particular, check colouring boundaries on the robin, junco and bluebird, and the woodpecker and jay wing patterns.
4. Test colouring with pencils or crayons. Inspect small spaces, filled markings and joined or broken boundaries. Pay attention to the Mourning Dove’s fine stems and the White-throated Sparrow, Carolina Wren and Starling details flagged by the 300-dpi screen. That screen does not certify any line width.
5. Try each of the six activity formats with a child ages 6–9 and a caregiver. Check whether the comparison diagrams, fictional notes, matching lines, swatches and answers are clear without a separate guide. Record confusion, unreadable labels, insufficient space and any misleading feature.
6. Record reviewer, date, page, finding and decision. Correct and recheck confirmed defects. User authorization is required before KDP upload or any proof purchase; local review is not KDP Print Previewer or physical-proof approval.
