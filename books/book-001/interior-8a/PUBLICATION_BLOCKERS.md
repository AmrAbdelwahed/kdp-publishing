# Publication blockers

1. Resolve the eight material artwork issues in `ARTWORK_QC_BACKLOG.csv`; complete a source-based human anatomy and identification review of all 30 selected images. A conceptual activity key is not a correction of the illustration.
2. Complete topic-specific scientific checks, local and seasonal applicability, age/sex/form choices, and human review of all text, diagrams and the 30 answer entries. Test the activities and labels with children ages 6–9 and caregivers.
3. Inspect an actual-size local print for margins, line weight, broken/merged colouring areas, small source text, filled dark patches and colouring space. Confirm font licensing and current KDP requirements; the automated screens and native raster sampling estimates do not certify print readiness.
4. Finish and approve the cover, credits, AI-content disclosure, current prices and royalties. Only after separate user authorization, use KDP Print Previewer and order/inspect a physical proof; approve final files and release separately.
