# Claim-specific scientific review

All 30 Cornell ID pages returned HTTP 200 on 2026-11-15 UTC, including B08 Red-breasted Nuthatch; direct source URLs, status, final URLs and retrieval timestamps are in `SOURCE_ACCESS.json`. B08's earlier 403 remains part of the preserved historical record. Cornell's Black-capped and Carolina chickadee comparison pages were also independently retrieved. The two species share the cap/bib/cheek clues used here; the comparison source also discusses range, wing edging and songs, which are not presented as a complete identification key in this draft.

`CLAIM_AUDIT.csv` records the exact 30 child-facing clue statements, a short source excerpt, source section, exact URL, retrieval timestamp, applicable intended sex/form/season, any second source, and an explicit artwork-match observation. These are direct text checks, not ornithologist review, certification of AI-generated anatomy, or proof of range in a child's locality. Main instructions that ask for a fact depend on the clue printed on that page; imaginary sightings are explicitly labelled. Pattern keys are schematic and do not claim to reproduce photographs.

`SCIENTIFIC_VERIFICATION.csv` retains the 150 check IDs and separate original topics. Taxonomy rows identify the previously retrieved Cornell overview for the common/scientific names. Identification rows refer to the specific clue actually used here. Anatomy/plumage rows remain pending image review; geography/season rows remain pending local-range review; activity suitability rows remain pending human and child-reader testing. A generic identification clue is not evidence for an unrelated habitat, taxonomy, anatomy or age-suitability check. Every row still requires appropriate human review.

No claim says that a bird is present in every yard or all year. Where a task concerns a particular male, age, season or named form, it says so in the clue. Sources describe natural variation; a coloured swatch or cartoon is not an identification guarantee. Prior externally supplied observations are preserved as such in historical files. The relevant chickadee shared-mark comparison and B08 head clue have now been independently checked against Cornell, rather than relying solely on those earlier reports.

Important mismatch risks are in `ARTWORK_QC_BACKLOG.csv`. No final scientific approval is claimed.
