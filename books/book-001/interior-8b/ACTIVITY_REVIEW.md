# Activity and age-suitability review

All 30 activities were screened against their printed clues, options, page-63 answers and available working space; see `ACTIVITY_REVIEW.csv` for the individual review. Their main tasks can be completed from information in the book, with an adult helping a child read if needed. No caregiver or child has tested them, so age suitability, independence and engagement remain provisional.

Focused changes preserve the six approved formats:

- Challenge answers now run **C, B, A, B, C**; comparison answers **B, B, A, B, A**; fictional field-note answers **C, B, A, C, B**. Reordered comparison diagrams move with their options. The page-63 key and coverage records are generated from the same activity data.
- Pigeon pattern cards now say “SAMPLE A/B” rather than printing the answer in each card label. Two challenge distractors are closer body-region alternatives, without changing the factual clues.
- The welcome page adds short bird-word hints; the flicker activity explains “underwing”; “shiny” is used in the Starling clue instead of “iridescent.” Cornell describes the summer appearance as iridescent. Vocabulary hints are child-friendly shorthands, not a complete technical glossary; human educator review is still required.
- Several direct-recall questions (notably the dove, wren, and tan-striped example) may be easy for older readers; other lookalike and matching tasks may need support for younger readers. Do not claim they have the right difficulty until tested. Filled art, tiny marks, visual comparison cards and source labels need the actual-size test.

Use `CHILD_CAREGIVER_FEEDBACK.pdf` with the `actual-size-print-packet.pdf`. Do not record fabricated results. All space dimensions in the CSV are layout allocations, not evidence of usable handwriting or colouring space in a physical print.
