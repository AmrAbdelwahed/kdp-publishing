# Interior preflight — review, not KDP approval

The 64-page review retains the approved page arrangement, all 30 selected vector-art pages, six activity formats, margins and typography. The only layout additions are a short welcome-page vocabulary hint; activities have the answer reordering and small usability edits described in `ACTIVITY_REVIEW.md`. `FINAL_PROPOSED_COPY.csv`, `ANSWER_KEY_COVERAGE.csv` and page 63 are generated from the same `ACTIVITIES.json`.

Checks include page count and sequence, even-left/odd-right viewing, letter dimensions, text bounds and text-span collisions, 30 distinct selected vector hashes, 30 matched clue statements and answers, source excerpts, 150 topic records, 30 pending reader-review records and embedded fonts. The tests compare every retained colouring page’s rendered pixels to the Milestone 8A PDF and verify both swapped comparison diagrams follow their labels. They also rebuild the older 8A design in a temporary directory and compare all 64 pages to protect the old builder’s default behavior.

`pdffonts` reports embedded DejaVu Sans and Sans Bold. Body text is about 12–12.5 pt, answer entries 11.5 pt and source/navigation labels about 9–10 pt. Font notices are in `FONT_LICENSE.txt`; final rights and credit review remains required. Automated text geometry does not prove that every shape, decorative line or printed mark is legible or unobstructed.

The 30 art pages have not changed since 8A. The prior `books/book-001/interior-8a/ARTWORK_SCREEN.csv` records actual vector placements and the 300-dpi raster screen: 1.62–20.5% of local maxima in those samples fell below the screening threshold, with B13 highest. It is not a measurement of every stroke, proof of a broken/closed colouring region, or a 0.75-point minimum certification. Source-raster sampling before vectorization was estimated at roughly 235.5–250.1 ppi at this placement; vector paths do not gain missing original detail. The final interior contains vector art, not embedded raster images.

The 20-page `actual-size-print-packet.pdf` begins with a right-hand cover, followed by eight complete even-left/odd-right spreads and book pages 62–64. It includes clean and complex art, lookalike and field-note tasks, several flagged illustrations, the answer page and the smallest source labels. Print on letter paper at 100%/actual size, long-edge duplex, starting with the cover. The separate 16-page original-versus-vector artwork comparison embeds the original PNGs for review and matches the final placement box; it is not a KDP interior. Use the accompanying print and child/caregiver sheets. No physical print or proof review has occurred.

Cornell source excerpts have been rechecked, but illustrations, geographical detail, glossary shorthand and age suitability need human review. The [KDP paperback guideline](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950), last retrieved during 8A on 2026-11-15 UTC, must be checked again before release. Automated checks are not KDP Print Previewer, print quality, scientific or publication approval.
