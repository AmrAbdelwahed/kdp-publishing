# Print at actual size — human review

1. Print the PDF at **100% / actual size**, with no fit-to-page scaling. Use letter paper and, for facing spreads, duplex with page 1 starting on the right. Check a ruler: the page should be 8.5 × 11 inches; home printers may clip nonprintable edges.
2. Inspect binding-side and outer margins, page numbers, title placement and all 30 pairings. Look for clipped, overlapping or nearly invisible marks.
3. Inspect every bird's head, bill, feet, wing and tail; compare original and vector art. Check the material species issues in INTERIOR_QC.md and mark uncertainty rather than guessing.
4. Colour sample areas with ordinary pencils or crayons. Check whether paths enclose the intended regions, spaces are usable and lines remain clear; test fine details and filled markings. Do not promise marker-bleed protection or single-sided artwork.
5. Read body text and 8.5–9 pt source/navigation labels at normal distance. Ask a caregiver or child in the target range to try activities; record confusing text, insufficient space, repetitiveness and misleading answers.
6. Record reviewer, date, pages, defects and decisions. Recheck corrections and current KDP specifications; obtain approval before ordering a proof or uploading anything.
