# Project brief
Date: 2026-11-10 (America/Toronto).

Goal: test whether one thoughtfully executed, original KDP paperback can support a sustainable, low-cost publishing business. Demand is a hypothesis, not proven. ChatGPT Pro supports creative work, research and editorial decisions; Codex handles repeatable processing, QA and eventual publishing preparation. The supplementary shared conversation is not a dependency and was not consulted.

Pilot: November 10–December 9, 2026. Budget ceiling C$50–100, with approval required for any spend. No paid APIs, keyword services, hosting, extra subscriptions or FBA inventory. Markets: Amazon.com and Amazon.ca.

Candidate: Backyard Bird Detectives: Color, Spot & Explore. Children 6–9; likely buyers are parents, grandparents and educators. Coherent colour–identify–observe progression across 30 candidate birds, with field marks, habitat prompts, outdoor observation options and regional caveats. Those individual educational devices are established practices; differentiation is execution, usability and accuracy.

Provisional format: 8.5 × 11 inches, paperback, black ink on white paper, no bleed; 64 pages: 30 colouring, 30 activities, 4 matter. Both sides are printed. Proposed list prices US$9.99 / C$13.99; user-reported royalties about US$3.15 / C$4.86 before advertising and other costs are unverified. No profitability or approval promise.

Success gates: traceable research, evidence-informed product decision, accurate and engaging age-appropriate content, original artwork with human QA, technically valid print files and explicit human release approval. Commercial success must later be measured by actual net receipts and costs, not assumed unit-sales estimates.
