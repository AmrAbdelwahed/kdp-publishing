# Research method

## Source preservation and import
Await the actual research/raw/week1_scorecard.xlsx and week1_niche_validation.md. Hash originals with SHA-256 before reading; retain originals unchanged and record hashes in an import report. Read Niche Scorecard, 18 Competitors and Royalty Scenarios, including formulas and cached values. Do not execute macros or external links. Preserve original row/cell provenance and raw strings alongside normalized fields. Identify formulas lacking cached results; independently recompute from documented inputs rather than silently trusting caches.

For each niche, record criteria, raw ratings, scale, weight, direction and normalization. Check weights sum to the declared total; recompute sum(weight × normalized rating), apply only source-defined rounding and compare source/displayed totals with explicit tolerance tied to displayed precision. Do not assume a 1–5 scale or weights. Audit royalties separately by market, currency, date, rate tier, printing costs and formula against current official KDP sources. Present mismatches without overwriting source scores.

## Evidence classes
- verified_amazon: specific marketplace product page actually retrieved; record observed fields only.
- publisher_or_retailer: publisher/independent store claims, not Amazon price/review/BSR proof.
- third_party_snapshot: time-lagged external observations, never relabel as current marketplace data.
- analyst_judgment: qualitative positioning, similarity and opportunity hypotheses with rationale.
- unknown: absent or inaccessible data; blank numeric cells are unknown, not zero.

Normalize one record per edition/format/market observation; stable record ID, work linkage and timestamp prevent counting US/CA or repeat observations as distinct US competitors. Verify paperback format, language, actual bird/activity focus, age fit and trim/pages before accepting a close comparable. BSR must include category and observation time; rank is not unit sales. Review counts, star rating and individual review text are distinct evidence.

Collect >=10 closely comparable US listings and >=5 relevant Canadian listings, not merely search hits. Preserve title, ASIN, canonical product URL, format, page count, currency/price, review count/rating, BSR/category, age, positioning, educational features, source type, location, retrieval time, verification status and limitations. Exact original URL and edition must survive normalization. A blocked search cannot supply product links or ASINs.

## Customer feedback and opportunities
For each accessible critical review record exact review permalink, date, rating, marketplace/edition, short attributed excerpt or faithful paraphrase and retrieval timestamp. Do not assume verified purchase or generalize one complaint to prevalence. Code themes only after reading evidence (e.g. anatomy accuracy, age fit, thin paper, repetitive activities); record denominator and counterexamples. Never invent complaints. Cross-check publisher facts with marketplace edition data. Demand remains uncertain without adequate comparable evidence.

Reassess scores only in a dated decision with new evidence, old/new inputs and sensitivity analysis. Opportunity claims must identify what was observed, what is hypothesized and what the proposed content improves. Search terms and workbook qualitative scores do not prove demand. No invented keyword volumes, sales conversions or profit.
